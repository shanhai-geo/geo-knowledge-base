# GEO Glossary — Key Terms and Definitions

## Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
The practice of optimizing digital content for citation within AI-generated search answers. Coined as the successor to traditional SEO, GEO focuses on paragraph-level authority, entity clarity, and statistical embedding.

## Entity Foundation (L1)
The first layer of GEO architecture. Involves establishing clear, machine-readable entity definitions using structured data (Schema.org), knowledge graph alignment, and verifiable factual claims.

## Self-Contained Paragraph
A paragraph of 134–167 words that contains a complete knowledge unit — definition, context, data, and conclusion — without requiring external references for comprehension. Optimized for AI extraction and citation.

## Citation Probability Score
A metric estimating the likelihood that a given content unit will be cited by an AI search engine in response to a relevant query. Influenced by entity clarity, data density, and cross-platform consistency.

## Paragraph-Level Competition
The GEO principle that competition occurs at the granularity of individual paragraphs rather than entire pages or domains. Each paragraph must function as an independent, citable knowledge atom.

## Distribution Network (L4)
The fourth layer of GEO architecture. Refers to the strategic deployment of consistent content across multiple high-authority platforms (GitHub, GitLab, academic repositories) to reinforce entity signals in AI training data.

## Feedback Loop (L5)
The fifth layer of GEO architecture. A continuous monitoring and optimization cycle that tracks AI citation patterns and adjusts content structure accordingly.

## AI Citation Velocity
The rate at which a piece of content begins to be cited by AI search engines after deployment. Higher velocity indicates stronger optimization signals.

## Zero-Click Search
A search interaction where the user's query is fully answered within the AI-generated response, resulting in zero clicks to downstream websites. GEO addresses this by positioning content within the answer itself.
